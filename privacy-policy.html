Fetchify Privacy Policy

App: Fetchify (com.vythera.fetchify) · Developer: Vythera · Effective: 31 October 2026

Summary

Fetchify is a download manager and browser. Your downloads, files, browsing history, bookmarks, settings and backups stay on your device. Vythera does not run servers that receive them. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Google Play handles purchases of Fetchify Premium.

Data that stays on your device

  • Downloaded files and the download list (links, file names, progress).
  • Browser tabs, history, bookmarks, cookies and site data, userscripts and ad-block lists.
  • Settings and backup files you export. Backups are saved only where you choose.

When you download or browse, the websites, trackers and torrent peers you connect to receive your IP address and requests, as with any browser or download app. Torrent downloads share pieces with other peers while they run.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads. Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

Fetchify Premium subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Google Play. Vythera receives purchase confirmations (order and product identifiers), not your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional filter-list downloads

When you update ad-block filter lists, Fetchify downloads them from the list publishers you enabled (for example GitHub). Those servers see your IP address.

Children

Fetchify is not directed to children under 13 and is listed for adults.

Your choices

  • Remove ads with Fetchify Premium.
  • Clear browsing history, cookies and site data in Settings.
  • Uninstalling Fetchify removes its private data; files saved to your folders stay until you delete them.
