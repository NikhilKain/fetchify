Fetchify Privacy Policy

App: Fetchify (com.vythera.fetchify) · Developer: Vythera · Effective: 20 November 2026

Summary

Fetchify is a download manager and browser. Your downloads, files, browsing history, bookmarks, settings and backups stay on your device. Vythera does not run servers that receive them; the only things Fetchify sends to Vythera are an anonymous usage check-in and any feedback you choose to send, both described below. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Google Play handles purchases of Fetchify Premium.

Data that stays on your device

  • Downloaded files and the download list (links, file names, progress).
  • Browser tabs, history, bookmarks, cookies and site data, userscripts and ad-block lists.
  • Settings and backup files you export. Backups are saved only where you choose.

When you download or browse, the websites, trackers and torrent peers you connect to receive your IP address and requests, as with any browser or download app. Torrent downloads share pieces with other peers while they run.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads. Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

Fetchify Premium subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Google Play. Vythera receives purchase confirmations (order and product identifiers), not your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage check-in

So Vythera can count how many phones use Fetchify, the app sends a small check-in to Vythera's admin server (hosted on Cloudflare) when it opens, every five minutes while it is open, and when it closes. Each check-in contains a one-way hash of your device's Android ID and the app's package name (the Android ID itself never leaves your phone), a random installation ID, a random session ID and how long the app has been open, your phone's maker and model, the Android version and the Fetchify version. The server's database stores the country the check-in came from, not your IP address, city or region. Check-ins never include links, file names, downloads, browsing or anything you type. They are used only to count devices and app usage, and are not shared or sold. Uninstalling Fetchify stops them. To ask for your device's records to be deleted, email us.

Feedback

Settings → Send feedback opens the Vythera feedback website in your browser. Nothing is sent until you submit the form there. The website stores what you enter (your name, email address, message and any attachments) so Vythera can read and reply to it, and Fetchify adds its version, your phone model and Android version to the link.

Optional filter-list downloads

When you update ad-block filter lists, Fetchify downloads them from the list publishers you enabled (for example GitHub). Those servers see your IP address.

Children

Fetchify is not directed to children under 13 and is listed for adults.

Your choices

  • Remove ads with Fetchify Premium, when it is offered in the app.
  • Clear browsing history, cookies and site data in Settings.
  • Uninstalling Fetchify removes its private data; files saved to your folders stay until you delete them.
